# Changelog

This file holds one section per release of elecdef, newest first.
Every change to the public surface gets a line under Unreleased until the release is cut.

## Unreleased

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-26)

- `throws` and `Throw`: the switched links that join a common pin to a break or make pin, with the state at rest.
- `poles` and `Pole`: one pole per run of switched or protective links that share a pin, with every pin of the pole and the line pin named from the LINE role or the marking numbering. A fuse path is a pole, a changeover is one pole, and unlinked pins and coil pins are in none.
- `check` reports a rest state on a link that is not switched.
- `check` reports a switched link whose stated rest state contradicts its break or make pin.
- `IssueCode.STRAY_REST_STATE` and `IssueCode.CONFLICTING_REST_STATE`: the codes for those issues; `IssueCode` has ten members.

## 0.4.0 (2026-10-24)

- `Block`: the base class of every block, now exported; it refuses a figure of the wrong kind at construction.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-24)

- `Unit.AH`, symbol "Ah", and `Kind.CHARGE`: battery and cell capacity in ampere-hours. It converts only within its own kind.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-23)

- `Rating.current`: rated current as `Ranges`, per current kind and conditions.
- `Rating.power_loss`: the power a part dissipates, with the load in `at`.
- `Contact.voltage`: the limiting voltage as `Ranges`, per current kind, like `Rating.voltage`. Break from 0.1.0: the field was one `Range`; it is now a tuple of them, and a bare `Range` is wrapped into a one-item tuple, so `Contact(voltage=one_range)` still builds but `contact.voltage` reads as a tuple.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-23)

- Value layer: Unit, Quantity, Range, Ranges, Role, At, pick, Pick, Issue and IssueCode, and the `elecdef.vocab` door with Kind, Current, Utilisation, CoilState and ConductorKind.
- Vocab and leaf blocks: the other vocabularies of `elecdef.vocab`, Connection, Body, Rating and Symbol, and the `elecdef.connections` door with its thirteen technology-only leaves.
- Pins and supply: the `elecdef.pins` door (Pin, PinRole, Side, Link, LinkKind, Marking, contact_pins) and the `elecdef.supply` door (SupplyInput).
- Switching: Contact, Coil, Relay, Contactor and ContactBlock; a contact or a coil stores only the pins its author gives, and a relay, contactor or contact block numbers its contacts on every read.
- Terminal, connector, computer and device: TerminalBlock and Device at top level, and the `elecdef.terminal` (Level), `elecdef.connector` (Connector) and `elecdef.computer` (Computer, Port) doors.
- PinRole.SCREEN, the last member of PinRole: the role of a cable screen pin.
- Tables: the `elecdef.tables` door, generated by `scripts/gen_tables.py` from `data/tables/*.toml`: CROSS_SECTIONS, AWG, AWG_METRIC, CORE_COLOURS, BREAKER_CURVES, BREAKER_CURRENTS, BREAKER_ICN, TRIP_TIMES, CABLE_VOLTAGES, OVERVOLTAGE_IMPULSE and E_SERIES.
- Conductor and cable: the `elecdef.conductor` door (Conductor) and the `elecdef.cable` door (Cable, Core, TypeCode); a Cable computes one pin per core and an SH pin with the role SCREEN when it has a screen.
- Protection: Overload, Fuse and Breaker at top level and the `elecdef.fuse` door (FuseHolder); an overload and a breaker number their auxiliaries on every read, and the fuse, fuse holder and breaker pin rows are proposed.
- PSU and PLC: PowerSupply and PlcCard at top level, and the `elecdef.plc` door (DigitalInput, DigitalOutput, AnalogIo, Channel, ChannelGroup); a power supply and a card store no pins and read them on every access.
- Product and check: Product(mpn, manufacturer, block, name=None) and check(block), which reports END_ORDER, DUPLICATE_MARKING, BAD_MARKING, DANGLING_LINK and UNKNOWN_CLASS for any block, and MISSING_FIGURE for the figures a block needs only inside a Product; the top-level surface is now 28 names.
- ChannelGroup refuses a template Channel that carries a number or pins, with TypeError; the group numbers its channels.
- check reports a marking-syntax issue once per authored pin, at the block that authored it (for example `contacts[1].own_pins[0]`), and DUPLICATE_MARKING once over the outermost block's full pin set.
- Docs: a worked-example guide page for the relay, contactor, overload and PLC card examples.
